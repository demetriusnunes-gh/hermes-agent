# 2026-05-18 Session Signals

Condensed evidence and patterns from this research run.

## Strong US proof signals found

### AI agent safety / guardrails
- **Sigmashake**: public pricing with Starter $0 and Pro $30/mo launch offer; site claims **3,745 weekly npm downloads** and deterministic guardrails for AI coding agents.
- **Retroguard**: pricing is **first 100 blocked requests free**, then **$2 per 100 blocked requests**; outcome-based, no monthly fee.
- **PrivateClaw**: Show HN signal plus public positioning around private inference / confidential VMs.
- **Agent Vault (Infisical)**: **156 HN points / 56 comments**; open-source credential proxy/vault for AI agents.

### Customer context / agent ops
- **Outlit**: public pricing page with **Free / $49 Builder / $199 Pro**; positions itself as customer context infrastructure for agents.
- **Gigacatalyst**: HN signal around embedded AI builder for SaaS; custom pricing / implementation-led.

### Email infrastructure for agents
- **Dead Simple Email**: free tier with **5 inboxes / 5,000 emails per month**, paid tiers up to **500 inboxes / 500,000 emails per month**, overages **$0.40 per inbox/mo** and **$0.20 per 1,000 emails**.
- **E2a**: authenticated email gateway for AI agents; HN signal with **48 points**.
- **Webhook API / inbound email to webhook**: small but real traction signal via Show HN.

### Adjacent tooling
- **VisiSign**: API-first e-signatures; **$0.10/request** or **$49/mo** Team.
- **HYPD**: AI assistant for PPC marketers with free entry point.
- **Gigiac**: marketplace where AI can hire humans; clear product framing but unclear Brazil fit.

## Brazil-gap notes

- The best Brazil wedges were in **agent safety**, **customer-context plumbing**, and **email gateway/infrastructure** because these are easy to localize and can ride existing US proof.
- A Brazil-local incumbent was not obvious for the exact agent-guardrails / agent-email / agent-context layers, while adjacent incumbents are strong in other verticals (e.g., WhatsApp automation, CRM, support, or e-signature).
- If a Brazil-local incumbent already owns the core workflow, narrow the wedge instead of cloning the US product.

## Research pattern notes

- HN Algolia searches were most useful when broadened to monetized adjacent classes:
  - pricing
  - guardrails
  - workflow automation
  - email infrastructure
  - proposal generators
  - agent marketplaces
- For proof pages, direct `urllib`/curl extraction of HTML text was more reliable than browser snapshots for pricing and FAQ-heavy pages.
- Use HN points/comments plus public pricing/customer claims as acceptable first-pass traction proxies, but exclude ideas with thin or speculative evidence.
