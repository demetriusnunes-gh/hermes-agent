# 2026-05-19 session signals

## What worked best
- HN/Algolia plus direct product pages still gave the highest-signal combination for AI microbusiness research.
- The strongest theme clusters were operational B2B layers, not broad AI platforms: voice/calls, recruiting screens, email infra for agents, billing/usage metering, procurement/tenders.
- Direct page extraction via browser/urllib is often more useful than relying on snapshot text alone when pricing or metrics are hidden in the DOM.

## High-signal proof points from this session
- **InterviewFlowAI**: live pricing at $59/$299/$499 per month, per-interview overage pricing, and public claim of trust by 100+ companies.
- **Flexprice**: live pricing and strong usage-scale claims; good proof that AI-native billing infrastructure can sell.
- **Dead Simple Email**: visible free tier and paid inbox tiers; strong signal for self-serve infrastructure around agent email loops.
- **Alto**: clear positioning for AI phone calls / hold handling / multilingual calling with a free starter and low-cost paid tier.

## Brazil-gap validation notes
- For recruiting/screening ideas, treat **Gupy** as an adjacent incumbent and narrow to a smaller wedge such as interview screening or agency workflow, not a generic ATS clone.
- For billing/monetization, Brazil has payment rails, but not much obvious AI-native metering/credits/usage tooling; good wedge is BRL-friendly usage billing plus invoice/export support.
- For agent email infra, the market looks more open; the main wedge is reliability + self-serve onboarding rather than feature breadth.

## Practical research heuristics
- If HN query results are sparse, broaden to monetized operational classes: pricing, guardrails, workflow automation, email infra, proposal generators, and marketplaces.
- When a JS-heavy page drops metrics from the snapshot, use direct HTML extraction before abandoning the candidate.
- Prefer ideas that already have both:
  1. explicit pricing, and
  2. some second signal like customer count, testimonials, or HN launch traction.

## Candidate shaping
- Best Brazil-first outputs should be framed as **small wedges** around proven US offers, not full clones.
- If Brazil already has a strong adjacent incumbent, exclude the idea or narrow the wedge until the incumbent is no longer the core workflow owner.
