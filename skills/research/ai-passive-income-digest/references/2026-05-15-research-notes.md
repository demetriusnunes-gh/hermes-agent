## HN / web search patterns that worked
- HN Algolia was sparse for exact long-tail prompts like "autonomous AI agent business ideas" and "AI micro SaaS".
- Broader queries worked better:
  - `Show HN AI`
  - `Launch HN AI`
  - `Show HN proposal`
  - `Show HN voice AI`
  - `Show HN email API`
  - `Show HN guardrails`
- Queries that surfaced usable leads included:
  - **Viche** — OSS private registry for agent communication
  - **Agensi** — curated marketplace for AI agent skills

  - **Rfp.ai** — source-cited AI RFP answers

## Brazil-gap takeaways
- **Restaurants/hospitality**: broad chatbot/ordering is crowded in Brazil; the better wedge is **missed-call recovery / voice receptionist** with WhatsApp handoff.
- **Healthcare**: scheduling/PMS is crowded; the better wedge is **AI front desk** layered on top of existing PMS/booking systems.
- **Licitações / procurement**: Effecti is strong and now advertises AI; avoid broad licitação automation. If used, narrow to auditable compliance/proposal copilot.
- **Proposals / RFPs**: generic proposal tooling is weakly localized, but a **PT-BR proposal copilot** for agencies and B2B services remains more plausible than a generic RFP clone.

## Practical lesson
- Strong US proof is necessary but not sufficient.
- Prefer narrow Brazil-first wedges that sit on top of existing local incumbents instead of cloning the US product one-for-one.
