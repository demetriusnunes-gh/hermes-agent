# Brazil AI passive-income digest notes

Session-specific evidence pattern for fast shortlist work:

## Strong proof signals used
- **OpusClip**: official homepage says it helps **10M+ creators** create and grow faster.
- **Photoroom**: official homepage says **300+ million downloads**.
- **ElevenLabs**: official site says **1M+ users**, **10,000+ businesses**, and **5,000+ voices**.

These are good enough as first-pass traction signals when the goal is to find already-working US services that can be rebuilt for Brazil.

## Brazil-fit filters that mattered
Keep only ideas that are:
- easy to localize to **Portuguese (PT-BR)**
- compatible with **Pix / boleto / card** payment behavior
- useful in **WhatsApp-heavy** SMB / creator workflows
- not blocked by obvious Brazilian regulation or local-market structure
- not already obviously dominated by a strong Brazilian incumbent

## Finalist patterns that passed
1. **AI short-form video clipping**
   - Strong because creator demand is obvious and the MVP can be narrow: upload long video -> clips + PT-BR captions.

2. **AI voice + dubbing localization**
   - Strong because Brazil has clear PT-BR demand and a small first version can target course creators / agencies: upload audio/video -> dubbed PT-BR version + subtitles.

3. **AI product photo / listing studio**
   - Strong because marketplace sellers benefit from fast, localized listing assets: upload product photo -> background removal + clean marketplace images + Portuguese bullets.

## Common exclusion reasons
- Enterprise-heavy tool with a long sales cycle
- Categories whose user behavior is too different in Brazil to translate well
- Ideas that are proven in the US but have no clear Brazil wedge
- Ideas with obvious local leaders already owning the niche

## Shortcut for future runs
If you need a tight shortlist quickly, prefer categories where the smallest Brazil-first version is a **single upload -> single outcome** workflow (clip, dub, photo/listing) rather than a broad platform.
