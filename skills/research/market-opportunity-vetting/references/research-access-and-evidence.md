# Research access and evidence handling

## Fallback when web search is unavailable
1. Try the configured search tool once and inspect the error.
2. If the failure is provider/configuration state, use an accessible search-engine results page through the browser or navigate directly to the candidate's official domain.
3. Prefer the official page's rendered DOM text over third-party snippets for traction claims.
4. Capture the exact claim, URL, and whether it is self-reported or independently corroborated.

## Evidence labels
- **Verified official traction:** customer/user/usage claim visible on the official product page, pricing page, or customer page.
- **Corroborated traction:** official claim plus reputable press, named customer, funding announcement, or other independent signal.
- **Market-gap inference:** conclusion from an incumbent sweep; use calibrated wording such as “no obvious dominant local leader found in this sweep.”

## Brazil incumbent checks
Search both Portuguese and English terms, including `site:.br`, and distinguish:
- Brazilian-native competitors,
- global products with Portuguese localization,
- agencies or service providers,
- unrelated entertainment or hobby products.

A crowded global category can still support a service wedge, but only if the wedge is explicit: PT-BR quality control, WhatsApp delivery, Pix/boleto billing, vertical specialization, or a single-upload -> single-outcome workflow.

## Session lesson
A browser search result may reveal stronger evidence than a compact page snapshot. For example, an official OpusClip page exposed a “16M+ creators and businesses” claim in rendered text. Use direct official URLs when search snippets are noisy, and avoid treating poor search retrieval as evidence of market absence.
