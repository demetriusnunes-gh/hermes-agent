## Brazil-fit calibration
- OpusClip, HeyGen, and Synthesia are already globally accessible, so the opportunity is a managed service rather than a generic clone.
- The strongest wedges are idiomatic PT-BR QA, WhatsApp delivery, Pix/card/boleto billing, vertical specialization, and fixed local turnaround.
- Incumbent searches were noisy and did not establish market absence. Use: “no obvious dominant Brazilian leader found in this sweep,” not “no competitors exist.”
- Meeting-notetaker clones were excluded because Fireflies already exposes PT-BR positioning and the category appears more crowded.

## Commercial evidence rule
Paid pricing demonstrates a monetization path, not verified revenue. Phrase the result as commercially validated by official adoption claims plus paid plans unless independent revenue reporting is available.
