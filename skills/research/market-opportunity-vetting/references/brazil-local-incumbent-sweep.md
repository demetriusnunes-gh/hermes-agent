# Brazil local-incumbent sweep notes

Use this quick check when vetting US products for Brazil rebuild potential.

## Fast exclusion categories
These categories often already have visible Brazilian-native or Brazil-localized options, so only keep a US leader if the wedge is unusually strong:
- WhatsApp / voice receptionist and lead qualification
- PT-BR meeting transcription, notes, and follow-up delivery
- Ad creative generation for performance marketing teams and agencies
- Product-photo / listing optimization for marketplaces and e-commerce sellers
- AI website builders
- Help desk / customer support AI
- Review-generation / reputation tools

## What to verify
1. Search for `site:.br` alternatives.
2. Look for Brazilian-native positioning, not just localized translation.
3. Prefer categories where the winning wedge is workflow simplicity, WhatsApp-native behavior, or local billing/compliance.
4. Exclude if the market looks like generic feature-parity competition.

## Good evidence signals
- Official homepage claims about users, customers, downloads, or scale
- Customer logos or named case studies
- Funding or valuation announcements from reputable press
- Official pricing/product pages showing a real paid product

## MVP bias for Brazil
Prefer a narrow, single-outcome workflow over a broad platform:
- upload -> transcript
- capture -> SOP
- job complete -> review request
- product photo -> marketplace-ready listing
