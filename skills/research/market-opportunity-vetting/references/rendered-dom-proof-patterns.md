# Rendered-page proof patterns

Use official rendered pages as primary evidence when search snippets are thin.

## What to capture
- Homepage scale claim: exact number and wording, e.g. “10M+ creators.”
- Pricing monetization signal: paid individual/team/enterprise tiers, price, billing unit, and API/custom-plan language.
- Customer proof: named customer logos, case studies, testimonials, or enterprise references.
- Product fit: supported languages, delivery channels, integrations, and export/workflow features.

## Calibration
- “10M+ creators” is official self-reported adoption, not independently audited revenue.
- A $15/month or $19/seat pricing page demonstrates a monetization path; it does not prove revenue.
- Named customers and reputable coverage corroborate traction but still do not establish profitability.
- Keep market-gap statements provisional unless supported by a broad local-market review.

## Brazil research pattern
For each finalist, separately check: `category + Brazil`, `category + Portuguese`, and `category + local competitors`. If results are noisy, report “no obvious dominant leader found in this sweep,” not “no competitors exist.” Then define the wedge as a concrete PT-BR, WhatsApp, Pix/card/boleto, vertical, or managed-service layer rather than generic feature parity.
