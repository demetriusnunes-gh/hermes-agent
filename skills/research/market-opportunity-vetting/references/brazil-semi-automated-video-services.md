# Brazil semi-automated video-service evidence pattern

Session note: for Brazil opportunity digests, two video workflows passed the proof/rebuildability gate when framed as localized services rather than generic SaaS clones.

## Short-form repurposing
- OpusClip homepage: https://www.opus.pro/
- Exact official claim observed: “Used by 16M+ creators and businesses.”
- Pricing: https://www.opus.pro/pricing
- Commercial signal observed: $15/month Starter, paid Pro and Business/API plans; Business section names Audacy, DOAC, and Vox Media.
- Brazil-first wedge: PT-BR captions and vocabulary review, one long upload to a fixed clip pack, WhatsApp delivery, BRL/Pix/card/boleto checkout.

## Video localization
- HeyGen homepage: https://www.heygen.com/
- Pricing: https://www.heygen.com/pricing
- Exact official claims observed: “100,000+ businesses,” “1,000+ reviews” on G2, and 175+ languages/dialects on the paid plan.
- Commercial signal observed: $29/month Creator and higher Pro/business/API offerings.
- Brazil-first wedge: English training or marketing video to reviewed PT-BR script, dubbed/lip-synced MP4, and WhatsApp/LMS delivery; obtain voice/likeness consent and handle customer data under LGPD.

## Evidence calibration
The official pages establish traction and monetization paths, not audited revenue. A noisy local search sweep should be reported as “no obvious dominant Brazil-local leader found in this sweep,” not “no competitors exist.” Global availability alone is not a Brazil opportunity; retain the candidate only when Portuguese quality, local billing, WhatsApp distribution, and a narrow managed-service layer create a concrete wedge.
