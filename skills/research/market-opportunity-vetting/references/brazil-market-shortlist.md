# Brazil market-shortlist notes

This reference captures the key selection rules and a few examples from a Brazil-focused AI rebuild search.

## Selection rules used
- Keep only products that are already working in the US and show traction, customers, revenue, or strong usage.
- Strongly prefer narrow rebuilds/adaptations over greenfield ideas.
- Exclude ideas that likely fit Brazil poorly because of regulation, local buyer behavior, payments, or localization friction.
- Prefer categories where Brazil has no obvious dominant local equivalent, or where the local wedge is clearly better UX/workflow.
- Limit the final shortlist to 3 ideas.

## Brazil-specific fit checks
- Portuguese output quality must be high, especially tone and formality.
- Pix and boleto should be considered for monetization if the product is SMB-facing.
- WhatsApp-native workflows matter for many Brazilian SMB use cases.
- Localized workflow beats generic translation.

## Example finalists that passed this kind of filter
- PT-BR meeting/call intelligence
  - US proof pattern: Fireflies-like tools with large user counts and strong adoption.
  - Why it fit: recurring SMB/sales use, low regulatory complexity, clear PT-BR wedge.
- PT-BR short-form video repurposing
  - US proof pattern: OpusClip-style products with very large creator adoption.
  - Why it fit: creator and agency demand, simple SaaS distribution, low regulatory complexity.
- PT-BR writing/tone assistant
  - US proof pattern: Grammarly-scale traction and monetization.
  - Why it fit: broad professional market, easy browser-extension distribution, strong localization value.

## Common exclusion patterns
- Enterprise-heavy AI agents with long sales cycles and weak SMB fit.
- Generic English-first creator tools without a sharp Brazil wedge.
- Categories already strongly owned by Brazilian incumbents unless the new product has a clearly better localized workflow.
