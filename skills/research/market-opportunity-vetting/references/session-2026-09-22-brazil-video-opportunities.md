# Session evidence note — Brazil video opportunity sweep (2026-09-22)

## Reusable evidence pattern
When web search is unavailable, use one diagnostic attempt, then an accessible browser search page only for discovery. Open official product pages directly and inspect rendered accessibility text; compact search snippets often omit the strongest claims.

## Evidence captured

### OpusClip
- Official homepage: https://www.opus.pro
- Rendered homepage claim: “Used by 16M+ creators and businesses.”
- Rendered product claims: long video to shorts, one-click publishing, API, team workspace, brand templates, and supported workflows.
- Pricing: https://www.opus.pro/pricing
- Rendered pricing: Starter $15/month; Pro pricing; Business custom pricing; free tier; paid features include captions in 20+ languages, publishing, limited API, and video dubbing.
- Brazil adaptation implication: do not rebuild generic clipping SaaS; sell a PT-BR managed workflow with human caption/tone QA, WhatsApp delivery, vertical templates, and Pix/card/boleto billing.

### HeyGen
- Official homepage: https://www.heygen.com
- Rendered homepage claims: 4.8/5 from 1,000+ G2 reviews; trusted by over 1,000,000 developers and leading companies; 175+ languages and dialects; 85% of Fortune 100.
- Pricing: https://www.heygen.com/pricing
- Rendered pricing claims: 100,000+ businesses; G2 #1 Fastest Growing Product of 2025; Creator $29/month; Pro $49/month; 175+ languages and dialects; API pricing available.
- Brazil adaptation implication: global availability means the wedge must be verticalized, consent-first PT-BR production rather than a generic avatar platform. Suitable initial verticals include real estate, education, recruiting, and franchises.

## Calibration and exclusions
- Paid pricing proves a monetization path, not independently verified revenue. Report these as official traction plus monetization, with revenue unverified unless third-party evidence is found.
- Search-result absence is weak evidence. Phrase local-market findings as “no obvious dominant Brazilian-native leader found in this sweep,” not “no competitors exist.”
- For Brazil, prefer a single-upload -> single-outcome managed service over a broad platform. State clearly that recurring service income is semi-automated, not fully passive.
- Exclude categories where the local incumbent sweep shows a strong Brazilian-native leader or where the localization wedge is only generic translation/UI localization.
