# Brazil AI passive-income candidate notes (2026-06-05)

Session-specific curation notes from a Brazil rebuild sweep.

## Search pattern that worked
- Start with a proven US product category.
- Verify traction from the official homepage first, then triangulate with reputable press/customer-logo pages when possible.
- Use DDG HTML / lightweight page extraction to collect product copy and proof signals quickly.
- Keep the final output to at most 3 ideas and force a Brazil-fit explanation for each one.

## Strong US proof signals seen in this sweep
- **Goodcall**: public homepage/marketing around high-volume voice-agent usage and large interaction counts.
- **Bland**: public marketing around enterprise adoption and high-value customer outcomes.
- **Fireflies**: public claim of **800k+ companies** using the product.
- **Otter**: public testimonials from named users plus recognizable brand logos.
- **AdCreative.ai**: public claim of **4.2M+ businesses** and **$35B+ in ad spend data**.
- **OpusClip**: public claim of **16M+ creators and businesses** using it.

## Brazil-fit observations
### 1) AI receptionist / WhatsApp voice agent
- Strongest Brazil wedge when positioned as SMB lead capture, booking, and FAQ handling.
- Fits Brazil because WhatsApp is already a core sales and support channel.
- Smallest viable version: PT-BR WhatsApp + phone agent for one vertical (salons, clinics, auto shops, home services).

### 2) Meeting notes / transcription / follow-up
- Strong Brazil wedge when localized for PT-BR and connected to action items, CRM, and WhatsApp delivery.
- Smallest viable version: join a meeting, transcribe PT-BR, summarize, and send action items to WhatsApp/email.

### 3) Ad creative generation for e-commerce and agencies
- Strong Brazil wedge when focused on fast creative variation, marketplace assets, and PT-BR copy.
- Smallest viable version: upload a product image and generate statics, headlines, and short video variants for Meta/TikTok.

## Exclusion reminders
- Exclude ideas that depend on a sales motion Brazil won’t support.
- Exclude ideas that are strong in the US but have no obvious local workflow wedge.
- Exclude broad platforms when a single-upload -> single-outcome workflow would be a better Brazil-first MVP.
